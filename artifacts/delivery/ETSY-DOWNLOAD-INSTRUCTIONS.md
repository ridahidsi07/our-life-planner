# Etsy Buyer Download Instructions

Thank you for your purchase of **Our Life Planner**!

Please review these instructions to download and open your files smoothly.

---

## ⚠️ Important Etsy Notice: Mobile App Limitation

> **Etsy does not support downloading digital files within the Etsy mobile app.**
> If you tap the download link inside the Etsy app on your iPhone or Android phone, it may show an error or fail to show a download button.

To download your files on a phone or tablet, you **must use a mobile web browser** (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge).

---

## Option 1: Downloading from a Computer (Recommended)

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox) and go to **[Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com)**.
2. Sign in to the Etsy account used during purchase.
3. In the top-right corner, click on your **Profile icon** (or **You**) and select **Purchases and reviews**.
4. Locate your order for **Our Life Planner**.
5. Click the black **Download Files** button to the right of the order.
6. Click **Download** next to each file:
   - `Our-Life-Planner-v1.0.0-Delivery.zip` (Complete delivery package)
   - `Our-Life-Planner-Getting-Started-Guide.pdf` (Printable reference guide)
7. Move the downloaded ZIP file to your preferred folder (such as `Documents` or `Desktop`).

---

## Option 2: Downloading on an iPhone or iPad (iOS)

1. Open the **Safari** browser app (do not use the Etsy app).
2. Go to **[Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com)** and sign in to your Etsy account.
3. Tap your **Account** icon in the top navigation bar.
4. Tap **Purchases and reviews**.
5. Find your **Our Life Planner** purchase and tap **Download Files**.
6. When prompted by Safari, tap **Download**.
7. Tap the blue downward-pointing arrow in Safari's address bar, tap **Downloads**, and select the downloaded file to view it in the Apple **Files** app.
8. In the Files app, tap the `.zip` file once to uncompress it into a standard folder.

---

## Option 3: Downloading on an Android Device

1. Open the **Google Chrome** app (do not use the Etsy app).
2. Go to **[Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com)** and sign in.
3. Tap your **Account** icon and go to **Purchases and reviews**.
4. Find your order and tap **Download Files**.
5. Once downloaded, open your device's **Files** or **My Files** app, navigate to **Downloads**, and tap the ZIP file to extract its contents.

---

## Option 4: If You Checked Out as a Guest (No Etsy Account)

If you made your purchase without logging into an Etsy account:

1. Check the email inbox associated with your order.
2. Look for an email from `transaction@etsy.com` with the subject *"Download your Etsy purchase from [Shop Name]"*.
3. Click the link inside the email to view your order receipt and download the files.
4. *Tip:* Check your Spam or Promotions folder if the receipt email does not appear within 5 minutes.

---

## How to Extract (Unzip) the Files

- **Windows 10 / 11:** Right-click `Our-Life-Planner-v1.0.0-Delivery.zip` and choose **Extract All...**, then click **Extract**.
- **macOS:** Double-click `Our-Life-Planner-v1.0.0-Delivery.zip`. macOS will automatically create an unzipped folder next to it.
- **iOS (iPhone/iPad):** Open the **Files** app, locate the `.zip` file in Downloads, and tap it once.
- **Android:** Open your **Files** app, tap the `.zip` file, and tap **Extract**.

---

## Re-downloading in the Future

Your Etsy digital purchase is permanently linked to your Etsy account. You can return to **Etsy.com > Purchases and reviews** at any time to re-download your files.

If you ever need assistance or run into any trouble downloading, please reach out to our team at **support@ourlifeplanner.app**. We are happy to help!
