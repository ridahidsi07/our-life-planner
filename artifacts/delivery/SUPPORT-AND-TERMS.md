# Customer Support & License Terms

Welcome to **Our Life Planner**! We are committed to providing dependable software and transparent support for your personal productivity system.

---

## 📬 Official Support Channel

For help, technical questions, or bug reports:

* **Official Support Email:** `support@ourlifeplanner.app`
* **Support Availability:** Monday through Friday
* **Target Response Window:** Within 24–48 business hours

### When Contacting Support:
To help us resolve your question quickly, please include:
1. Your operating system (e.g., Windows 11, macOS Sequoia, iOS 18).
2. Your web browser and version (e.g., Chrome 124, Safari 18, Edge 122).
3. A description of what you were doing and what occurred.
*(Tip: In the planner, you can open **Settings > Support & Contact** and click **Copy Prepared Message** to automatically assemble non-personal diagnostic details.)*

---

## 🎯 Scope of Support

### What Is Included:
* Guidance on setup, first-time configuration, and module options.
* Troubleshooting local storage or browser compatibility questions on supported platforms.
* Assistance with JSON backup exports, restores, and data migration.
* Investigating and resolving reproducible software defects or bugs.

### What Is Not Included:
* Certified financial, medical, or dietary advice (the Money, Workout, and Meal modules are personal self-tracking tools only).
* Custom code modifications or third-party browser extension troubleshooting.
* Instant 24/7 live telephone or chat support.
