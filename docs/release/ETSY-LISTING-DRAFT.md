❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Q: Do I need any special software like Notion or Excel?
A: No! Our Life Planner runs directly inside any standard web browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox). You do not need to buy, install, or learn any third-party spreadsheet or database software.

Q: Will my data be lost if I clear my browser history?
A: Your planner data is saved in your browser's persistent storage. We recommend exporting a 1-click JSON backup weekly from Settings > Data & Storage to your iCloud, Google Drive, or hard drive for permanent safekeeping.

Q: Can I use it on both my phone and my laptop?
A: Yes! You can use the hosted web application on both devices. You can also transfer your data at any time via 1-click JSON backup/restore, or activate the built-in Cloud Sync using your purchase claim code.

Q: Is this a monthly subscription?
A: Absolutely not. This is a one-time purchase that includes lifetime personal use of version 1.0.0.
